Tip: paste a screenshot straight into the box (Mac: Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4, Windows: Win+Shift+S), or drag one in. You can also highlight an error on any page, right-click, and choose “Ask FA Helper about this”.

Screenshots are sent as they are. ID masking can’t hide names or IDs inside images, so crop or cover student information first.
