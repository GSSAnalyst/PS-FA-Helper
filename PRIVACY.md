# Privacy Policy: PeopleSoft FA Helper

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

PeopleSoft FA Helper ("the extension") is a Chrome side-panel assistant for financial aid staff.
This policy explains what data it handles. The short version: **the developer does not collect,
receive, store or sell any of your data.** The extension has no server of its own.

## What the extension stores on your computer
Saved in Chrome's local extension storage, on your device only:
- Your Claude API key
- Your chosen model and the "mask ID numbers" setting
- Office notes you type into Settings

Your conversation is kept in memory only while the side panel is open. It is cleared when you
click **New** or close the panel.

## What is sent, and to whom
When you ask a question, the extension sends the following **directly from your browser to
Anthropic** (api.anthropic.com), the provider of the Claude AI model, using your own API key:
- The text of your question, and any text you highlighted and sent with "Ask FA Helper about this"
- Any screenshots you attach
- Your office notes
- Earlier messages in the same conversation, for context

Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy
(https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and the commercial terms of your API account.
To answer, Claude may search and read public pages on a fixed list of government and Oracle
documentation sites. Those searches run on Anthropic's servers, not from your browser.

Searches of the bundled PeopleBook happen entirely on your computer.

## Student information
The extension is built to minimize student data exposure:
- By default it replaces text that looks like Social Security numbers or 7–10 digit ID numbers
  with placeholders **before** anything is sent.
- It **cannot** mask information inside screenshots. Crop or cover student names, IDs and other
  personal information before attaching a screenshot.

You are responsible for following your institution's policies, including FERPA, when deciding
what to send.

## What the extension does not do
- It does not read pages you visit. It only sees text you choose to highlight and send.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
- It does not sell or transfer data to third parties, apart from sending your questions to
  Anthropic as described above.
